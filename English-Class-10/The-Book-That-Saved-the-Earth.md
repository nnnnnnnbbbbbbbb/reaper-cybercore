# The Book That Saved the Earth — Class 10 CBSE

**Author: Claire Boiko**

> Original revision notes. This is not a reproduction of the NCERT chapter.

## Overview
This humorous science-fiction play is set in the future. A Martian expedition discovers a book of nursery rhymes on Earth. Think-Tank, the arrogant Martian commander, misunderstands the rhymes and becomes so frightened that he cancels the invasion.

## Complete summary
A Historian from the twenty-fifth century introduces an incident from the twentieth century: a book of nursery rhymes saved Earth from a Martian invasion. The Martian commander Think-Tank plans to conquer Earth and sends a crew to investigate. The crew includes Captain Omega, Lieutenant Iota and Sergeant Oop. They land in a library and discover books, which they do not understand.

The crew contacts Think-Tank. He first imagines that books are sandwiches and orders the crew to eat them. When this fails, he decides that books may be communication devices and tells the crew to read one aloud. The book is Mother Goose. Oop reads the rhyme about Mistress Mary. Think-Tank misunderstands the garden objects as evidence of dangerous human technology.

The crew then reads the rhyme about the cow jumping over the moon. Think-Tank wrongly concludes that Earthlings can send animals into space. Finally, Oop reads the Humpty Dumpty rhyme. Think-Tank notices that Humpty Dumpty resembles him and becomes frightened by the description of the character falling and not being put together again. He imagines that Earthlings know about him and can destroy him.

Think-Tank cancels the invasion and escapes to Alpha Centauri. The Earth is saved. Later, Noodle becomes the Martian leader and relations between Mars and Earth become friendly.

## Main characters
- **Historian:** Narrates the incident from the future.
- **Think-Tank:** Arrogant Martian commander who repeatedly misunderstands the book.
- **Noodle:** Think-Tank’s apprentice; sensible, tactful and intelligent.
- **Oop:** A member of the Martian crew who reads the book.
- **Iota:** A member of the Martian expedition.
- **Omega:** Captain of the Martian expedition.

## Events in order
1. The Historian introduces the story of how a book saved Earth.
2. Think-Tank sends an expedition to investigate Earth.
3. The crew lands in a library and finds a book.
4. Think-Tank first mistakes books for sandwiches.
5. He then orders the crew to read the book.
6. The Mistress Mary rhyme is misunderstood as evidence of weapons.
7. The cow-and-moon rhyme is misunderstood as space technology.
8. The Humpty Dumpty rhyme frightens Think-Tank because the character resembles him.
9. Think-Tank cancels the invasion and escapes.
10. Noodle later leads Mars towards friendly relations with Earth.

## The three nursery-rhyme misunderstandings
- **Mistress Mary:** Think-Tank imagines that the garden items mentioned in the rhyme are dangerous devices or weapons.
- **Hey Diddle Diddle:** He thinks the cow jumping over the moon proves that Earthlings can send animals into space.
- **Humpty Dumpty:** He sees a resemblance between Humpty Dumpty and himself and fears that Earthlings can destroy him.

## Central idea and themes
The play uses comedy to show how ignorance and overconfidence can lead to absurd conclusions. It celebrates books and knowledge, and suggests that sensible interpretation and communication are better than arrogance and fear.

## Key terms
- **Invasion:** an attack by entering a place with force.
- **Expedition:** a journey made for a particular purpose.
- **Apprentice:** a learner who works under an experienced person.
- **Nursery rhyme:** a short traditional poem for children.
- **Interpretation:** an explanation of meaning.
- **Conquer:** to take control of a place by force.

## Exam practice
**What was the book that saved Earth?** Mother Goose, a book of nursery rhymes.

**Who was Think-Tank?** The arrogant supreme commander of Mars.

**Who was Noodle?** Think-Tank’s intelligent and tactful apprentice.

**Why did Think-Tank think books were sandwiches?** He did not understand what books were and guessed from their appearance.

**Why did the Humpty Dumpty rhyme frighten him?** Humpty Dumpty resembled Think-Tank, and the rhyme described a fall from which the character could not be restored.

**How was Earth saved?** Think-Tank misunderstood the rhymes as proof of Earth’s great power and cancelled the invasion.

## Message and title
The title is appropriate because the Mother Goose book accidentally prevents the Martian attack. The play reminds readers that books contain knowledge, while ignorance and arrogance can produce foolish decisions.
